S.G.O.201-Leonard, Esther E.,                                                         MCG.

               WAR DEPARTMENT
       OFFICE OF THE SURGEON GENERAL
                 WASHINGTON
                     May 18, 1918.

Dr. Esther E. Leonard,
     6410A Michigan Avenue,
          St. Louis, Missouri.

Madam:-

       I am directed by the Surgeon General to inclose herewith con-
tract which should be dated and signed by you in the presence of a
witness, whose signature should appear on the line provided there-
for, and be returned to this office, in the inclosed franked envel-
ope. No contract should be returned addressed to any person other
than "The Surgeon General, U. S. Army."   One copy of the contract
should be retained by you.

       You will note that the contract covers a period of one month.
This does not imply that it will necessarily terminate at the end
of that period.  It can be continued indefinitely if such exten-
sion is mutually agreeable.

       Immediately upon signing this contract you should telegraph
this office that you have done so, when steps:will be taken to have
you properly placed on duty.

       Do not enter upon your duties, nor perform any travel under
your contract until you have received from The Adjutant General of
the Army, either by telegraph or mail, an order to do so.

       Seven cents per mile is allowed as commutation for travel per-
formed under proper orders, and upon the completion of any such trav-
el, mileage voucher should be obtained from the nearest Quartermaster,
which you should fill in, making no extension in the money column how-
ever, and then return the same together with two copies of the order
directing the travel, to the Quartermaster for payment.

       At the end of each month during your service under your contract,
you should obtain pay voucher from the nearest Quartermaster and re -
turn it properly prepared, together with your copy of contract, which
will be returned to you by the Quartermaster after he has made nota-
tion of payment thereon.

       Just as soon as you have completed the duties for which your con-
tract was made, and have returned to your home under proper orders, you
should notify this office of that fact by telegraph, when your contract
will be annulled.

                                                       C. W. AYARS
Incs.                                      Major, Sanitary Corps, N. A.
